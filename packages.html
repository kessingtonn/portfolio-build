Questions

Before you book

How do we hold a date?

A signed agreement and a 25% booking fee hold the date. The balance is due two weeks before the shoot, or monthly in arrears on retainers.

Do you travel?

Constantly. UK travel is included in every wedding package. Outside the UK we quote flights and accommodation at cost — no mark-up, no day rate for travel days.

Can we combine photo and film?

Yes, and most clients do. The Signature and Brand Story tiers already include both. For any other combination, tell us what you need on the enquiry form and we will price it as one package.

When do we get everything back?

Sneak peeks within 48 hours. Full wedding galleries in six weeks, portraits in seven to fourteen days, brand and UGC work in ten to twelve days. Rush delivery is available as an add-on.

Who owns the footage?

You get full personal-use rights on delivery, and commercial licensing is written into every brand and UGC package. We keep the right to show selected work in this portfolio unless you ask us not to.

What if the plan changes?

Dates can be moved once, free, subject to availability. Cancellations inside 30 days retain the booking fee. It is all written plainly in the agreement — no fine print games.
