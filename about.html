The studio

A small crew, deliberately.

Zangrid Studios is four people who shoot, cut and deliver everything themselves. No account managers, no handoffs, no one on your wedding day you have never met.

Our approach

We would rather miss a shot than stage one.

The studio started with wedding films and grew into brand work because the same instinct applies: find the real moment, light it honestly, and cut it before it outstays its welcome.

In practice that means we plan hard so we can improvise on the day. We arrive early, we know the running order, and we have already worked out where the light will be at four in the afternoon. Then we mostly disappear — and what comes back looks like the day you actually had, not a version of it arranged for the camera.

Shooting stills and motion together is the other half of it. One crew, one set of decisions, one look across the photographs and the film. It costs less than hiring two teams and it argues with itself far less.

Kit

What we shoot on

01

Cameras

Full-frame mirrorless bodies, dual card slots, always two per operator. Everything backed up twice before we leave.

02

Glass

Fast primes for the quiet moments, a workhorse zoom for the ceremony, and a macro for the details that matter to you.

03

Sound

Lavaliers on the vows and the speeches, a backup recorder on the registrar. Your film is cut to real audio, not library music alone.

04

Light

Natural first. Small continuous LEDs when the room gives us nothing — never a flash in the middle of your first dance.
